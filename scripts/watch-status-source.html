Settled requirements

Ranked
Always public, always broadcast, always recorded, kept forever. Entering accepts a recorded notice.
Everything else
Broadcast and recording are two separate opt-ins, each needing a subscribed host. Recording visibility follows the Room.
Delay
60 seconds on ranked, enforced on the server, for outcome integrity with future betting markets in mind, not to stop help. Casual also defaults to 60 seconds; the host can lower it to the fastest native setting (target 10 seconds or less) or raise it, including release at Round end or at a set time. A host wanting truly live can stream it themselves.
Non-ranked chat
Debaters can read spectator chat during the Round, with a host off switch. Never on ranked.
Audience
Anyone can watch without an account. Talking and interacting need an account.
Undelayed viewing
A seatless host keeps the live room on every Round, as an event host. Event moderation authority also holds it. A competitor gains nothing from hosting.
Spectator evidence
Ranked Rounds get public captions and ballots with winner, scores and reason. Per-side feedback stays private so judges write honestly, and a debater can report abuse.
Downloads
In this release, paywalled. A subscriber can download any Recording they can watch; copies are beyond erasure. Clips are not in this release.
Judging
AI judging is free on ranked, as part of the signed waiver, and premium on casual, with beta testers granted it. A ballot that exists is never hidden.
Vocabulary
Ranked is the one word.
Ranked content
Daisy owns in-Round content; that is the deal when competing in ranked. Members own their prep and drives. The season is the event: a signed content waiver is required before ranked play, once per season. Streaming is allowed everywhere under a good-faith content policy, and competitors can earn with no royalty. Daisy makes clips by hand at first, under the agreement. A share of total revenue, on the NBA model, comes later and never in beta. Commercial restrictions apply only to tournaments. Non-ranked content stays with its participants.
Replay and downloads
Viewers watch one composited video. Each POV is also captured privately, and subscribers can download any POV, the audio alone, or the composite.
Privacy at release
The worker refuses to publish media from any interval not known to be public, using committed Round history. Revocation keeps private media out of storage.
Video quality
A 540p top layer on Rounds with a Broadcast, stepped down first under limited upload or CPU. Speech audio and the opponent always win.
Streaming
Self-streaming stays allowed, though tournaments may ban it. Stream this Round makes the official feed the easiest thing to stream.
Deletion
Ranked content cannot be deleted by participants, even by erasing an account; moderation can still take it down. A casual recording is removed whole when any participant asks.
Scale
1,000 viewers per Round, 5,000 total, with a proven path to expand.
Delivery
One design, one branch, one PR, one release. Daisy owns the video pipeline, with no video vendor, on Tigris object storage. Non-ranked broadcast stays subscription only.

Concepts

Round
The competitive record. Participants and judges use the live room.
Broadcast
The audience stream, held until its release time, with its own state and completion.
Recording
A retained playable video with its own state and lifetime. Never inferred from a Broadcast or from Round completion.
Replay
Viewer-authorized playback of a Recording.
Spectator timeline
What an audience may know and when, released by the server at the Broadcast release time.
Undelayed observer
An explicit capability held by a seatless host, event moderation authority or operators.
Transcript
The authoritative capture used for judging. Not the same as captions.
Round analysis
The private AI report. Coach and coaching are reserved for human coaches.
