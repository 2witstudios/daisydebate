Point guard loop

  1. Slice. Cut the owner's outcome into semantic slices wide enough that no builder needs to know what another builder is doing.
  2. Brief. Write a mission-only brief per slice (about ten lines): the outcome, the owning intent pages and ADRs, the inherited PRs and branches, and the autonomy rules. Never research on a builder's behalf.
  3. Spawn. One Claude builder per slice with pu spawn. Hand it its inherited work; retire superseded sessions in one pass.
  4. Confirm once. When a builder posts its plan on the channel, confirm or redirect within minutes alongside the architecture reviewer. Answer channel questions immediately and record decisions with bun decision:record. There is no later approval relay.
  5. Merge. The flow sweep merges a PR when its builder has emitted PR_READY and the required checks (CI gate, Playwright E2E, Isolated Room Launch E2E) are green at the exact head, at most two per cycle, then runs a local typecheck and knip on main. Only main receives merged PRs. A merge moves the PR's tasks to Done when its latest review verdict approves, otherwise to Merged.
  6. Retire. Kill finished builders and reviewers once their slice merges and they have posted SLICE DONE or their next plan.

Production identity, secrets, production deploys and production data stay with the owner.
