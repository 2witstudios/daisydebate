A member in the Youth lobby signs this once per season, in place of the [ranked content waiver](ranked-content-waiver.md), before Ready on their first ranked round of that season. Daisy records the account, the version and the time. It grants Daisy no licence: a minor's content is reused only under a media release a parent, guardian or club gives.

## The deal

Ranked rounds are always public, and they are broadcast and recorded. Anyone can watch them live and replay them on Daisy, and they show on your profile and in the archive. If every capture machine is busy, a round can run without a broadcast or recording; the debate and its result are not affected. Competing in one is the deal. If you do not want this, play casual.

## Kept on Daisy

A ranked round's video, transcript and result stay on Daisy as part of the ladder's record, including after you close your account. You, or your coach or parent, can ask for a round's recording to be taken down at any time. [COUNSEL: retention of a minor's ranked record after account closure.]

## No licence to your content

Signing this gives Daisy no ownership of what you say or show, and no right to clip it, to use your name or likeness in other media, or to let anyone stream or post it. Daisy offers no download of your ranked rounds and no Stream this Round feed of them. That changes only while a media release from your parent or guardian, or from your club, covers every youth debater in the round. [COUNSEL: whether public viewing under the lobby's rules needs a parent's consent in any state.]

## Payment

This waiver promises no payment.

## What stays yours

What you say and show, your prep material and your drives stay yours, ranked rounds included.

## Other rounds and events

A non-ranked round is not covered: its content stays with its participants. A tournament may have its own per-event terms.
