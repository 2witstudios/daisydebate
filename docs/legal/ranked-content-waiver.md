A member signs this once per season, separately from the Terms, before Ready on their first ranked round of that season. Daisy records the account, the version and the time.

## The deal

Ranked rounds are always public, always broadcast and always recorded. Competing in one is the deal. If you do not want this, play casual.

## In-round content

Daisy owns what is said and shown in a ranked round, including its video and transcript. [COUNSEL: the form of the grant: assignment, exclusive licence or other.] It stays after you close your account, because ranked season rounds are qualifying artifacts for tournaments.

## Clips and other media

Daisy may edit ranked rounds into clips and other media, and use your name and likeness in them, to broadcast and promote ranked play. This includes clips made by hand from this release on. [COUNSEL: name-and-likeness wording, by jurisdiction, and for members under 18.]

## Payment

This waiver promises no payment. Any revenue share would be a future program, not part of the beta, and is stated only as counsel approves. [COUNSEL: whether to mention it at all.]

## What stays yours

Your prep material and drives stay yours, ranked rounds included.

## Streaming and posting

You and anyone else may stream and post ranked rounds under the [ranked content policy](ranked-content-policy.md), with no royalty, and Daisy may restrict or take down ranked content. The ranked ladder has no exclusive broadcast, no streaming limit and no commercial restriction on competitors.

## Other rounds and events

A non-ranked round is not covered: its content stays with its participants. A tournament may have its own per-event terms.
