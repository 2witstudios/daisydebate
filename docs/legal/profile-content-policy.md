# Profile content policy — draft, not served

**Draft for owner approval (PROF-13). Not reviewed by counsel and not served.** It becomes part of the served terms only after the owner approves the text and it is added, versioned, to `apps/web/src/features/legal/agreements.ts`. [COUNSEL: placement in the terms, and the appeal route.]

## What you can put on your profile

Your display name, bio and photo are public: anyone who opens your profile, and other members in their lists, can see them. Your username stays your name on the record: on ballots, standings, rooms and chat.

## What is not allowed

Don't use a name, bio or photo that:

- pretends to be another person, a judge, a team or Daisy itself;
- is sexual, or shows nudity;
- harasses, threatens or demeans anyone, or attacks people for who they are;
- shows graphic violence or self-harm, or encourages it;
- advertises, spams, or tries to send people somewhere else;
- shares someone else's private information, or a photo of someone who did not agree to it.

## How photos are checked

Every photo is scanned for malware and has its hidden data (such as location) removed. An automated check then reads it. A photo the check is unsure about waits for a person at Daisy before anyone else sees it; until then only you see it, marked "In review". A photo the check refuses is not saved.

## Reports and removals

Anyone can report a profile's photo, display name or bio. A person at Daisy reviews each report. If something breaks this policy we remove it, and repeated or serious breaches can restrict your account under the terms. You can always clear your own display name, bio or photo, even while your account is restricted. [OWNER: whether a member is told when content is removed, and how.]

## When your account closes

Closing your account deletes your display name, bio and photos. Your username and your competitive record stay, as the terms describe.
